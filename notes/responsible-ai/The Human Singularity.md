A singularity is a point, moment, or condition at which normal rules, equations, and predictions break down and no longer apply. With the rapid development of AI, humanity risks moving toward a technological singularity where algorithms handle all innovation and skill, leaving us as passive observers. In doing so, we risk losing our capacity for critical thinking, reducing our focus entirely to the end goal rather than the value of the journey. I consider that threshold to be a critical singularity moment for humankind-a point where we forfeit our degree of freedom, creativity, and preseverance in exchange for convienience.

Today's models often lack explainability and interpretability for human operators. While humans are inherently reasoning beings, current ML model-based solutions tend to black-box the decision-making process, focusing exclusively on the final result. Although these models can uncover complex patterns much faster than is humanly possible, we miss a crucial truth: the act of finding a pattern is a journey. Along that path of discovery, we encounter diverse auxiliary insights that help us understand not just our primary target but entirely new concepts waiting to be explored.
